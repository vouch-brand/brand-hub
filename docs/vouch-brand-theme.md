# Vouch Brand Guidelines — Colours & Typography

For building a Google Slides brand theme.

## Brand aesthetic (for tone/mood reference)

Calm, clear, modern, human. Low-contrast, open, editorial rather than "typical SaaS." Paper-like neutral backgrounds, generous whitespace, a confident-but-not-stuffy serif. Think "more Kindle, less iPad" — grounded and real, not glaring or clinical.

## Typography

### Typefaces

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Google Fonts equivalent | System fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headings / display | Martina Plantijn (serif) | Regular, Bold | Source Serif 4 | Georgia, serif |
| Body / UI | Inter (sans-serif) | Regular, Medium, SemiBold | Inter (native) / Roboto | Arial, sans-serif |

Note: Martina Plantijn is a licensed/self-hosted font, not on Google Fonts — use **Source Serif 4** as the closest free equivalent for Google Slides.

### Type scale (document/print reference)

| Style | Size | Weight | Kerning | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | 48px | Medium | -3 | 90% |
| H2 | 36px | Medium | -3 | 90% |
| H3 | 28px | Medium | -3 | 100% |
| Body 1 | 20px | Regular | 0 | 150% |
| Body 2 | 18px | Regular | 0 | 150% |
| Body 3 | 16px | Regular | 0 | 150% |

Spacing rules: line height ≈ 1.5x body text size · heading kerning -3 · body kerning 0 · paragraph spacing ≈ 1.5x font size.

## Suggested Slides theme mapping

- **Background:** Sand Light (#F9F6F1), or Sand Lightest (#FDFDFB) for elevated/card surfaces
- **Dark background variant:** Sea Blue Dark (#001D39)
- **Primary accent / headings / CTAs:** Sea Blue Mid (#44607B)
- **Body text:** Dark Neutral (#212121)
- **Secondary accents:** Sky Blue Mid (#9DC4CE), Sun Mid (#F7B139), Green Mid (#688F7A), Purple Mid (#796BB2) — for charts, tags, highlights
- **Heading font:** Source Serif 4 (or Georgia as fallback)
- **Body font:** Inter (or Roboto as fallback)
